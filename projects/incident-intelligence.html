Real-Time Log Incident Intelligence Platform

Client: Optum, Alpharetta | Resource: Softech International Inc - USA

A centralized real-time incident intelligence and log analytics platform using machine learning and LLM-driven reasoning to improve operational visibility and accelerate incident resolution. Machine learning models support anomaly detection, incident clustering, and severity classification on high-volume log streams, while LLMs generate structured incident summaries, root-cause insights, and remediation recommendations under human-in-the-loop control.

The platform is built on Kafka, stateful stream processing, Spark MLlib, and production MLOps practices for log ingestion, enrichment, correlation, and model-backed decision support.

Impact

  • Reduced alert noise through anomaly detection, clustering, and intelligent incident correlation.
  • Improved MTTD by 35% and reduced MTTR by 25-30%.
  • Enabled LLM-assisted incident summaries, RCA insights, and remediation recommendations.
