Signal Hub & Identity Linkage

Client: Experian, Alpharetta | Softech International Inc - USA | Feb 2022 - Jan 2024

A real-time intelligent data analytics platform integrating enterprise credit, fraud, and identity data sources into a unified ML and deep learning architecture for entity representation and linkage intelligence. Deep learning models generated address embeddings and customer vectors for similarity learning, entity resolution, and linkage across disparate systems.

These vector representations powered credit risk scoring, fraud network construction, feedback-driven fraud intelligence, and explorable datasets for business and compliance teams.

Focus Areas

  • Entity resolution and identity linkage across enterprise data sources.
  • Deep learning-based address embeddings and customer vectors.
  • Risk scoring, fraud detection, anomaly detection, and analytics-ready datasets.
