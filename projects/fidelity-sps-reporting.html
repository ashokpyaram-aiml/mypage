Fidelity SPS Global Reporting

Client: Fidelity Investments | Resource: Infosys - US & India

Built a self-service financial reporting platform for Fidelity Stock Plan Services, supporting restricted stock plans, stock options, and employee stock purchase plans.

  • Migrated reporting from on-premises BI to AWS with Snowflake as the cloud data warehouse and Tableau for reporting.
  • Configured Oracle Goldengate Kafka connectors for CDC and created Java batch extraction from mainframe databases.
  • Built Spark transformations to generate Parquet for batch and realtime data handling.
  • Reduced maintenance costs by 30% and improved report usage through better access and reporting UX.
