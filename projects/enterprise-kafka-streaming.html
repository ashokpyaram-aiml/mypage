Enterprise Kafka Streaming Integration

Role: Solution Designer / Kafka SME | Client: Danske Bank | Resource: Coforge - India | Period: Feb 2021 - Jan 2022

Built an enterprise real-time streaming and integration platform on Confluent Platform and Confluent Cloud to support mission-critical financial workflows and high-throughput data movement. The system was tuned through configuration optimization and horizontal scaling to improve throughput, reduce latency, and ensure fault tolerance at scale.

Enterprise systems were integrated using Kafka Connect source and sink connectors, including Debezium CDC, IIDR, IBM MQ Series, mainframe systems, Salesforce, S3, and Snowflake. End-to-end security with SSL and OAuth, plus Prometheus and Grafana monitoring, improved platform reliability and reduced implementation costs by approximately 30% compared to legacy approaches.

Focus Areas

  • Confluent Cloud and Kafka Connect integration architecture.
  • CDC, MQ, Salesforce, S3, and Snowflake source/sink connector patterns.
  • SSL/OAuth security, observability, performance tuning, and Terraform-based automation.
