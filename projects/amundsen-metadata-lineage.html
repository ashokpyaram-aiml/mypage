Amundsen RAG Metadata Discovery & Lineage

Role: Team Lead / AI & Data Engineering | Client: Experian, Alpharetta | Resource: Softech International Inc - USA | Period: Feb 2022 - Jan 2024

Implemented a RAG-enabled enterprise metadata discovery and lineage platform using Amundsen, with strong ownership of retrieval, search-engine design, and business-friendly dataset discovery. Built an intelligent retrieval layer using custom indexing, BM25 relevance tuning, semantic retrieval patterns, field boosting, synonym expansion, fuzzy matching, and query rewriting to improve metadata search precision and recall.

The platform improved dataset discoverability and governance adoption by enabling explainable RAG-style search across datasets, schemas, business terms, and lineage relationships.

Architecture: metadata ingestion from Snowflake and enterprise sources to Amundsen indexing to Elasticsearch/BM25 and semantic retrieval to Neo4j lineage graph to analyst search experience.

Focus Areas

  • RAG-style retrieval, custom IR ranking, and relevance tuning for enterprise metadata search.
  • Metadata indexing for datasets, schemas, lineage relationships, business terms, and retrieval context.
  • Snowflake and Neo4j integration for end-to-end dataset visibility.
