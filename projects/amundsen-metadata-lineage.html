Amundsen Metadata Discovery & Lineage

Client: Experian, Alpharetta | Softech International Inc - USA | Feb 2022 - Jan 2024

Implemented an enterprise metadata discovery and lineage platform using Amundsen, with strong ownership of information retrieval and search-engine design. Built an intelligent search layer using custom indexing, BM25 relevance tuning, field boosting, synonym expansion, fuzzy matching, and query rewriting to improve metadata search precision and recall.

The platform improved dataset discoverability and governance adoption by enabling business-friendly, explainable search across datasets, schemas, and lineage relationships.

Focus Areas

  • Custom IR ranking and relevance tuning for enterprise metadata search.
  • Metadata indexing for datasets, schemas, lineage relationships, and business terms.
  • Snowflake and Neo4j integration for end-to-end dataset visibility.
